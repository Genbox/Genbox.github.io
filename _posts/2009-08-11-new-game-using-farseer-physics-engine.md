We have about 1300 daily pageviews and 200 downloads each day over at the [Farseer Physics
Engine](https://github.com/Genbox/VelcroPhysics) project. We also have quite a few users posting their
games for others to review and to show off how they used the engine.

A lot of the games are good, but Ninjic surpassed my expectations. The graphics are from [UP
Studio](https://web.archive.org/web/20100201234130/http://upstudiodp.com:80/) and the designer and a guy teamed up to create a ninja-style game.

See the [video of Ninjic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=78xjHnicYCA) on YouTube.
