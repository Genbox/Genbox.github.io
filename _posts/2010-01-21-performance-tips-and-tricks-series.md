development. Use constants and loop manipulation.

[Part 3](/2009/08/performance-tips-and-tricks-part-3.html) – Information about method inlining.

[Part 4](/2009/09/performance-tips-and-tricks-part-4.html) – Boxing and unboxing, more on exception
