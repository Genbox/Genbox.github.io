I looked around for some statistics to get an idea on the amount of visitors that read my blog. I
was surprised that nothing like that existed by default. Google is the owner of Blogspot and they
are known for their detailed analytics of the internet and reliable site tracking, so I thought that
it was a joke that Blogspot did not have a statistics module by default.

**Google took the right path.**

So why not include a statistics module by default? I’ll tell you why; the best sites in the world
are defined by their functionality and ease of use. *Not* including a default statistics
module and letting the users choose what they like is very clever and gives your users an extremely
flexible service.

I personally went with [Google Analytics](http://www.google.com/analytics/). There are several
[other services](https://statcounter.com/blogger/) that track site statistics. The choice is up
to you.
