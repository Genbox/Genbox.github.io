A new game called Square off just got posted on the Farseer Physics Engine forums. It is created by
[Gnomic Studios](http://www.gnomicstudios.com/blog/) and has entered the [Dream Build
Play](https://web.archive.org/web/20100106120613/http://www.dreambuildplay.com:80/main/default.aspx) competition.

It features great graphics, multiplayer and a huge fun-factor. Take a look at [this
video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=phMS3B3U5Tk) to see what I mean.
